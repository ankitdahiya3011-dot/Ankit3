Privacy Policy

Bluebird Espresso respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect personal information when you visit our café, contact us, or use any services we make available.

Who is responsible for your information Bluebird Espresso is responsible for the personal information described in this policy. If you have questions or wish to exercise your privacy rights, contact us using the contact details provided on our website or at our café.

Information we may collect Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect your name and contact details, information you provide when making an enquiry or placing an order, and details of your transactions. If you use a website or online service we provide, we may also collect technical information such as your IP address and information about how you use that service.

How we use your information We may use your information to provide products and services, process orders and payments, respond to enquiries, manage bookings where available, and handle requests or complaints. We may also use it to maintain business records, improve our services, protect our café and customers, and meet our legal obligations. We will send marketing messages only where permitted by law. You can opt out of marketing at any time by following the instructions in the message or contacting us.

Our lawful bases for processing Under UK data protection law, we rely on one or more lawful bases to use personal information. These may include performing a contract with you, taking steps you request before entering into a contract, complying with a legal obligation, pursuing our legitimate interests in operating and improving our business, or obtaining your consent where required. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

Sharing your information We may share information with service providers who help us run our business, such as payment, ordering, booking, delivery, website or IT providers, where applicable. They may use the information only to provide services to us and must protect it appropriately. We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect our rights, customers or business. We do not sell your personal information.

International transfers If a service provider transfers personal information outside the United Kingdom, we will take steps required by applicable data protection law to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

How long we keep information We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including to meet legal, accounting or reporting requirements. The relevant period depends on the type of information and why it was collected.

Cookies and similar technologies If we operate a website, it may use cookies or similar technologies to make the site work, understand how it is used, or support other functions. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Where required, we will ask for your consent before using non-essential cookies.

Keeping information secure We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights Depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, ask us to correct or erase it, restrict or object to its use, or request a copy in a portable format. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To make a request, contact Bluebird Espresso using the details provided on our website or at our café. You have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Children’s information Our services are not intended to collect personal information from children unless necessary to provide a service and handled in accordance with applicable law. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

Changes to this policy We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be made available through our usual customer information channels.
